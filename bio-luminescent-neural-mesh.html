Bio Luminescent Neural Mesh

Michael Rapoport
Polaritronics, Inc.
Abstract
A neural interface system comprising: a biocompatible mesh structure configured for injection and self-weaving integration within neural tissue; and a plurality of bioluminescent agents integrated with the biocompatible mesh structure, wherein the bioluminescent agents are configured to selectively emit photonic signals in response to processed data, the photonic signals being of sufficient intensity and proximity to stimulate cortical neurons to generate a perceived visual overlay.

Claims

A neural interface system comprising: a biocompatible mesh structure configured for injection and self-weaving integration within neural tissue; and a plurality of bioluminescent agents integrated with the biocompatible mesh structure, wherein the bioluminescent agents are configured to selectively emit photonic signals in response to processed data, the photonic signals being of sufficient intensity and proximity to stimulate cortical neurons to generate a perceived visual overlay.

Description

Bio-Luminescent Neural Mesh: A biocompatible, injectable photonic interface that weaves itself into the user's cerebral cortex, allowing for direct visual overlays of data processed through biological light-emitting enzymes, eliminating the need for external screens or ocular implants.
